Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Rich CV Maker : Resume Builder is made by Rich Solutions. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Your CVs and photos stay on your phone

The CVs you write, your photo and the PDFs you make are saved only on your device. We do not receive, see or store them. The "Clean background" photo tool works on your phone, without the internet.

Optional account

If you choose to log in, Google Firebase Authentication keeps your email address and name so you can sign in. You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Account > Manage > Delete my account.

Optional Google Drive backup (Pro)

If you turn on Drive backup, a backup file of your CVs is saved in a private app folder of your own Google Drive. Only this app can read that folder. We do not have access to it.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details.

Children

The app is meant for adults and job seekers aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.
